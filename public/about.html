About RiskSim AI

Built different.

Three founders under 18. No funding. No office. Just a problem worth solving.

Mission

Why we built this

Mid-market companies importing globally have always been flying blind. The tools that actually work — real-time risk intelligence, landed cost modeling, AI supplier audits — cost $50,000+ a year and were built for Fortune 500 enterprises with dedicated analyst teams. Everyone else was stuck with spreadsheets, Google alerts, and gut instinct. We built RiskSim AI to close that gap. Palantir-level supply chain intelligence, rebuilt from the ground up for the companies that actually need it.

Vision

Where we're going

Supply chain disruptions cost mid-market companies billions every year — not because the risks weren't there, but because nobody saw them coming in time. We're building the platform that changes that. Every importer, every sourcing team, every procurement manager deserves the same intelligence that the world's largest companies take for granted. That's what RiskSim AI is becoming.
